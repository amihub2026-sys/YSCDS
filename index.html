QUICK ANSWERS

Before you nominate.

Common questions applicants may have before starting the recognition process.

Who can apply for a YSCDS record?

Individuals, teams, schools, colleges, academies, institutions and organisations can submit achievements for consideration.

What kind of proof may be required?

Depending on the achievement, applicants may be asked for photos, videos, witness information, identity details and supporting documents.

Can traditional arts such as Silambam be recognised?

Yes. Sports, traditional arts, cultural performance and other qualifying achievement areas can be considered through the relevant category.

Is the website ready for online applications?

The current version is a static presentation website. The nomination form can later be connected to email, WhatsApp or a backend workflow.
